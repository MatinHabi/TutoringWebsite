About me

I'm Matin. I study Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at Adelaide University, and I tutor Maths, Physics, English and Coding for Years 4–12, online and in person in Adelaide.

Experience

With Elevate Education, I've run study-skills workshops for classes of 30+ students at schools across South Australia and in Brisbane, working with more than 650 students.

How I teach

In one-on-one lessons I break difficult topics into clear, manageable steps and adapt to the way you learn.

For senior students, lessons focus on SACE assessments, exam technique and lifting your ATAR. For younger students, the focus is on solid foundations and the confidence to give every question a go.

After every lesson you get a short summary and a progress update, so students and parents always know where things stand.
